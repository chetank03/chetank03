### Hi there! <img src="https://emojis.slackmojis.com/emojis/images/1536351075/4594/blob-wave.gif" width="25" alt="waving hand" />

I'm [**Chetan**](https://linkedin.com/in/chetan-kodeboyina-b229a8219/), a software engineer in New York City.

I'm finishing an MS in Computer Engineering at NYU in May 2027, and I did my bachelor's in Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence at King's College London.

I'm drawn to large scale systems where performance is part of the product, the kind where a missing index or an extra copy per node turns into seconds a user actually feels.

What I enjoy most is working out why something is slow, since the cause is rarely where anyone expects. I also like working below the framework layer, on compilers and firmware, and I have spent more time than is reasonable on binomial lattices.

A few things I've built: a [C++ option pricer](https://github.com/chetank03/american-option-lattice-engine), [STM32 firmware](https://github.com/chetank03/parkinsons-motion-monitor) that detects tremor on device, a [viewer for polymer notation](https://github.com/chetank03/bigsmiles-viewer), and a [compiler that targets LLVM-IR](https://github.com/chetank03/functional-language-compiler-llvm).
